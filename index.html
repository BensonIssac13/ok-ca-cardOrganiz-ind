Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026

The short version

  • We do not collect any personal data. We have no servers and no accounts.
  • Everything you add, including photos, stays on your device.
  • Text and image recognition run on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
  • Deleting the app removes all of its data.

This policy explains how Oaklawn Casino Card Organizer ("the app") handles information. The app was built to work without collecting anything about you.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask you to create an account or sign in. The developer receives no data from the app, and the app makes no network requests.

Information stored on your device

Whatever you choose to enter stays in the app's private storage on your iPhone or iPad:

  • Loyalty cards: program, venue, member name, card number, tier, notes and any card photo you add
  • Points history you log for each card
  • Membership details you enter, such as name, member ID, home venue, renewal date and your host's name and phone
  • VIP benefits you mark as used
  • Your horse's profile: name, measurements, pedigree, race record and any photo you add
  • Reminders you set

None of this is sent to us or anyone else. It is included in your device backups (iCloud or computer) according to your own backup settings.

Permissions

  • Camera. Used only when you scan a loyalty card or photograph your horse. Photos are kept in the app on your device and never uploaded.
  • Photos. Used only when you choose a picture from your library. The app sees only the photo you pick.
  • Notifications. Used only for the reminders you set, such as a card or membership about to expire. They are scheduled on your device; no push server is involved.

Each permission is optional. You can change it at any time in iOS Settings, and the rest of the app keeps working.

On-device recognition

Reading a card number and checking a horse photo use Apple's Vision framework, which runs entirely on your device. No image or text leaves your phone.

Tracking, analytics and advertising

The app does not track you across apps or websites, contains no advertising, and uses no analytics or crash-reporting services.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

You can delete any card, entry, reminder or horse inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing to request from us.

Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles information changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.

Independence

Oaklawn Casino Card Organizer is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Oaklawn Racing Casino Resort or any other casino, racetrack or loyalty program. It does not connect to any loyalty program and cannot check or change real point balances. The app does not offer gambling or betting of any kind.

Support

Help & questions

Answers to the most common questions. If yours isn't here, write to us.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start adding cards. There is no sign-in, and everything stays on your device.

The card number wasn't read correctly. What can I do?

Use good light, hold the card flat and fill most of the frame. You can always edit the number by hand after scanning.

Does the app show my real point balance?

No. The app doesn't connect to any loyalty program. It keeps the points history you log yourself so you can track your progress.

I don't get reminders.

Check Settings → Notifications → Oaklawn Casino Card Organizer and make sure notifications are allowed, then confirm the reminder is switched on in the Club tab.

Are the horse scores a prediction?

No. They are a statistical estimate from the measurements you enter, for planning and fun. They are not a guarantee of results and not betting advice.

Will my data move to a new iPhone?

Yes, if you restore your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one. The app has no cloud account of its own.

Can I gamble or bet in the app?

No. The app is an organizer only. It has no gambling, betting or real-money features.
